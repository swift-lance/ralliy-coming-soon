What’s coming

A freelance marketplace, rebuilt for trust.

Ralliy replaces guesswork with evidence, and slow payouts with stablecoins — so the right talent finds the right job, and gets paid the moment work is approved.

AI matching engine

A weighted scoring model ranks freelancers against every job using milestone parity, verified credentials, and tooling fit — no proposals required.

Verified credentials

Skill scores are tied to evidence — jobs completed on Ralliy, real portfolio work, or unverified claims — so clients see what’s real, not what’s written.

Open bounties

New freelancers prove their skill the fair way: by shipping. Two successful bounties unlocks verification and access to AI-matched jobs.

Project health checker

Every milestone is scored on completeness, relevance, communication, revisions, and timeline. Green, yellow, or red — spot trouble before it grows.

Stablecoin payouts

Get paid in USDC anywhere in the world, alongside traditional bank payouts. Fast, low-fee, and powered by Stripe — no crypto wallets to wrangle.

Escrow & milestones

Funds are held safely on Stripe Connect and released only when each milestone is approved — protection for clients, certainty for freelancers.
